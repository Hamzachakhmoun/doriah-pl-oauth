Privacy Policy — Doriah PL

Last updated: October 2026.

Doriah PL is a private internal tool used solely by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Ads account via the Google Ads API for the single purpose of reading advertising spend and campaign performance into a private dashboard.

Data use: Google account and Google Ads data is used only to display the owner's own advertising data to the owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for any other purpose.

Data storage: Authentication tokens are stored securely and are never exposed. Data is retained only as long as needed for the dashboard to function.
